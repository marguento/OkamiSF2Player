# HQ Orchestral: reverb and chorus per instrument

How much each instrument of the [HQ Orchestral SoundFont Collection](https://musical-artifacts.com/artifacts/817) v3.0
sends to the reverb and the chorus, read from the .sf2 itself (`TailTest <file> sends`). It's what
plays with CC91 and CC93 at 0; raising them in a clip adds up to 20 % more. The plugin shows the
same figure in each effect panel's header.

195 instruments: 125 send to the reverb, 47 to the chorus, 66 send nothing (dry).

A range (`6–30 %`) means the send differs between the instrument's notes or layers. `—` is
nothing. The SoundFont stores only the amount: the reverb and chorus themselves are the plugin's
(its knobs), or Ableton's with **Effects to separate outputs**. Bank 128 is percussion.
